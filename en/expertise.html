Our projects

Case studies

Oria Terranea

Silk scarf house — founding brand

Oria Terranea was born from an intuition: that silk could carry far more than a pattern — that it could tell the story of the Mediterranean. Its shores shaped by civilisations, its light inherited from millennia of encounters, the tears and joys this sea has absorbed since Antiquity. Each piece is an invitation to wear this heritage. Artification allowed us to build, from the very origin, a brand whose perceived value reflects the depth of its cultural roots.

Hexagone Padel

Padel rackets — brand identity

Turning a functional sports equipment into a symbol of belonging and distinction. We defined a brand identity rooted in French elegance — the heritage of tennis, the rigour of craftsmanship, the nobility of the sporting gesture. A product that became iconic of the French touch, carried by a visual universe where lifestyle transcends performance.
